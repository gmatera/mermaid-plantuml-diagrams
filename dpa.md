*Last updated: 17 November 2026.*

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the agreement under which the customer ("Customer") uses
the Atlassian Marketplace app **Mermaid & PlantUML Diagrams – Sharp Export for Confluence** (the "App")
provided under the Steadyseam brand by **Sasso di Matera Giuseppe S.a.s.** (Via Clefi 2, 20146 Milano (MI),
Italy, VAT IT13043470965) ("Provider"). It applies to personal data processed by the App on behalf of the
Customer, as required by Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and by similar laws.

## 1. Subject matter, nature and purpose

The App renders Mermaid and PlantUML diagrams on the Customer's Confluence Cloud pages and stores SVG and PNG
images of diagrams saved in the editor for display and for PDF and Word exports. Processing consists of
storing and retrieving that information in Atlassian Forge storage, only to provide the App. Diagrams are
drawn in the user's browser. The App does not contact any service outside Atlassian.

## 2. Personal data and data subjects

- **Data subjects:** any person whose personal data the Customer's users write into diagram text.
- **Personal data:** any personal data that appears in diagram text stored in Confluence macros; SVG and PNG
  images derived from that text. The App does not store Atlassian account IDs, names or e-mail addresses.
- No special categories of personal data are intended to be processed.

## 3. Duration

For as long as the App is installed, plus the retention periods in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
On uninstallation, Atlassian deletes the data stored by the App according to its data retention policy for
Forge apps. Diagram text that remains in Confluence page content is under the Customer's control.

## 4. Provider obligations

The Provider:
1. processes personal data only on the documented instructions of the Customer, given by installing,
   configuring and using the App;
2. ensures that anyone authorized to process the data is bound by confidentiality;
3. implements appropriate technical and organizational measures (Article 32 GDPR). The App runs only on
   Atlassian's Forge platform with no servers of the Provider, draws diagrams in the user's browser, sanitizes
   every SVG before display, refuses image saving unless the user can edit the page, ties each image to its
   page and macro, and validates inputs on the server;
4. engages Atlassian (Forge hosting) as the only sub-processor, which the Customer authorizes, and will
   inform the Customer of any intended change, giving it the opportunity to object;
5. assists the Customer, as far as possible, in responding to requests from data subjects and in meeting its
   obligations under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR;
6. notifies the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the
   App;
7. deletes the personal data at the end of the service as described in section 3;
8. makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and allows for audits in
   the form of written questionnaires answered within 30 days.

## 5. International transfers

Personal data stays in Atlassian's cloud (Confluence page content and the site's Forge storage, with the data
residency of the site). The Provider does not transfer it elsewhere and does not receive or see customer
data.
